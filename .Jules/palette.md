## 2026-03-05 - Pygame Pause Readability
**Learning:** Rendering white text directly over a busy simulation background reduces contrast and makes text hard to read.
**Action:** Always provide a semi-transparent dark overlay behind critical centered HUD elements (like a PAUSED state) to ensure sufficient contrast and visual hierarchy.
## 2025-05-17 - Dynamic Toggles and Persistent End States
**Learning:** Users experience cognitive friction when toggle labels are static or simulation ends abruptly.
**Action:** Use dynamic toggle labels reflecting the next action and implement persistent end-state overlays requiring manual acknowledgment to preserve visual context.
